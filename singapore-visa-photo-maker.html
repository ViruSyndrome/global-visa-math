📸 Photo Requirements

  • Size: 35mm wide × 45mm tall. White background only.
  • Face coverage: Face (from chin to top of head) must take up 70–80% of the frame.
  • No Glasses: Singapore ICA requires glasses to be removed for all pass and visa photos.
  • Background: Plain white — no patterns, no shadows on the background.
  • Expression: Neutral expression, both eyes fully open, looking directly at the camera.
  • Recency: Photo must have been taken within the last 3 months.
  • Lighting: Even lighting on your face — no harsh shadows. Facing a window in daylight works best.
