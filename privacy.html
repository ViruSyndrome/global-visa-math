Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

GlobalVisaMath is designed with a strict privacy-first model. We do not require any signup, account registration, or email verification to access our tools. No travel histories, passport details, test dates, ages, or immigration status data are ever sent to our servers or stored in any remote database.

2. Client-Side Execution

All calculations, date parsing, and score summaries are executed entirely within your web browser using standard client-side JavaScript. Once you close or reload the browser tab, the data processed in your session memory is permanently discarded.

3. Google AdSense & Analytics

To support the maintenance and hosting of this free service, we display advertising spaces through Google AdSense. These services may collect standardized browsing metrics:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Links to External Resources

Our pages contain links pointing directly to official governmental portals (such as USCIS, IRCC, and the European Union Lex portal). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or contents of those external government sites.

5. Contacting Us

For questions regarding our privacy practices, you can contact us at privacy@globalvisamath.com.
