    Understanding the 90/180 Rule

    Under the Schengen Borders Code, non-EU citizens may stay in the Schengen Area for a maximum of 90 days within any 180-day period. This is a "rolling" 180-day window, meaning the assessment looks backward 180 days from any given day of your stay. Both the day of entry and exit are counted as full days within the territory.

    Recent Expansions: Croatia joined the Schengen Area fully in 2023. Additionally, Bulgaria and Romania joined for air and sea borders in March 2024. Stays in these nations now count toward your Schengen 90-day limit.
