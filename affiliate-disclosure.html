Affiliate Disclosure Policy

Last Updated: September 2, 2026

1. Overview

GlobalVisaMath is committed to transparency and full disclosure of our business practices. This page explains how we monetize our site and our affiliate relationships.

2. Our Core Principles

  • No personal data collection: All our calculators (Schengen 90/180, Canada CRS, etc.) run entirely in your browser. We do not store, log, or transmit your visa, travel history, or personal data to any server.
  • Tool accuracy first: Our calculation logic is based on official government regulations (EU Regulation 2016/399, IRCC CRS point allocation, USCIS timelines) and is not influenced by affiliate partnerships.
  • Clear monetization: We earn revenue through affiliate commissions on third-party services. This does not change your cost when using these services.

3. Affiliate Partners

GlobalVisaMath may earn affiliate commissions when you purchase through our links to the following services:

iVisa (Visa Application Service)

  • What they do: iVisa helps travelers apply for visas online (Schengen, travel visas, etc.) without visiting embassies.
  • Commission structure: Per-application affiliate commission (varies by visa type).
  • Our relationship: We feature iVisa links on our Schengen calculator and visa photo maker pages. Clicking "Check Visa Requirements" or "Apply for Visa" directs you to iVisa's site.
  • Impartiality: Our Schengen calculator results and accuracy are independent of this partnership. We recommend iVisa because it is a recognized, established visa application service.

SafetyWing Nomad Insurance

  • What they do: SafetyWing provides travel and medical insurance for nomads, tourists, and expat travelers, including Schengen-compliant insurance (required for Schengen visa applications).
  • Commission structure: 10% recurring commission on policies sold via our affiliate links.
  • Our relationship: We feature SafetyWing insurance on our Schengen calculator and visa photo maker pages. Schengen visas require €30,000+ in medical coverage; SafetyWing meets this requirement.
  • Impartiality: We recommend SafetyWing because it is a Schengen-approved insurance provider and a natural complement to visa applications (sequential customer need: you need a visa first, then you need insurance to apply).

4. No Bias or Exclusive Partnerships

  • We do not hide or downplay competitor options.
  • Our calculator results (Schengen 90/180 status, CRS points, etc.) are not designed to push you toward any specific service.
