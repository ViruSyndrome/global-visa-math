    The 90/180 Rule and Greece

    Greece including its islands is fully within the Schengen Area. Days in Santorini, Mykonos, Crete, or Athens all count toward your 90-day limit.

    The Schengen Area treats all 27 member countries as a single zone. Whether you enter at Athens airport or travel overland from a neighbouring country, every day counts toward the same rolling 90-day limit.

    Frequently Asked Questions — Greece

    Does visiting Greece use up my Schengen days?

    Yes. Greece is a full Schengen member. Every day you spend in Greece counts toward your shared 90-day limit across all Schengen countries.

    Can I enter Greece if I have only 10 Schengen days left?

    Yes, but you can only stay for 10 days across the entire Schengen Area before you must leave. Use the calculator above to check your exact remaining days.

    Do I need a visa to visit Greece?

    It depends on your passport. Citizens of many countries (US, UK, Australia, Canada) can visit Greece visa-free for 90 days. Citizens of India, China, and many others require a Schengen visa. Check your country's requirements at the official embassy website.
