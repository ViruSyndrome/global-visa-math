How to Improve a CRS Score of 540

  • Improve language scores — IELTS or CELPIP CLB 9+ gives maximum language points
  • Add a job offer — A valid LMIA-backed offer adds 50–200 points instantly
  • Provincial Nomination — A PNP nomination adds 600 points, near-guaranteeing an ITA
  • Canadian education — A 2-year diploma or degree adds significant human capital points
  • Spouse language score — Improving your spouse's English/French score adds adaptability points
