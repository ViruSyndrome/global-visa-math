Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. No account — calculators run in your browser

GlobalVisaMath does not require signup, login, or email verification. Trip dates, CRS inputs, Green Card dates, J-1 program dates, and photo crops are processed in your browser with client-side JavaScript. We do not operate a backend that stores those calculator inputs in a database.

Closing or reloading the tab clears that session data from memory. This is not the same as “no data is ever collected anywhere”: third-party scripts on the page can still receive standard web analytics and advertising signals, as described below.

2. Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-5KJNDPS0EG) to understand traffic and feature use. In addition to standard page views, our tools may send custom events such as calculator runs (for example Schengen day counts, CRS scores, Green Card remaining days, or J-1 checks). Event payloads are aggregate usage metrics, not passport scans or uploaded photos.

Google may set cookies or use similar identifiers subject to Google’s privacy policy. You can limit ad personalization via Google Ads Settings and review Google’s data practices on Google’s sites.

3. Google AdSense

Some pages load Google AdSense (ca-pub-2959862133855422) to help fund hosting. AdSense and its partners may use cookies or device signals to serve and measure ads. Users may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.

EEA/UK: A Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) must also be enabled in the AdSense console for visitors in those regions; site-level Consent Mode defaults are not a substitute.

4. Error monitoring (Sentry)

We load Sentry’s browser SDK to capture client-side errors so we can fix broken pages. Session Replay sample rates are set to 0. Sentry may receive technical details such as URL, browser type, and stack traces when an error occurs.

5. Fonts, CDNs, and other third parties

  • Google Fonts — pages request Inter/Outfit (or similar) from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com.
  • cdnjs / other CDNs — some tools load libraries (for example Cropper.js) from public CDNs such as cdnjs.
  • Affiliate / partner links — outbound links (for example travel eSIM offers) may use affiliate tracking parameters when you click through. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

Those third parties process requests under their own policies when your browser fetches their resources.

6. Camera and photo makers

Visa photo tools can use your device camera via getUserMedia and crop images locally. Photos are not uploaded to GlobalVisaMath servers as part of the crop/download flow. Granting camera permission is optional and controlled by your browser.

7. External government links

We link to official portals (IRCC, USCIS, EU resources, and similar). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.
