About GlobalVisaMath

Our Mission

Navigating travel rules and immigration criteria is complicated. Whether calculating the European 90/180 rule, determining Canada Express Entry CRS scores, or tracking complex J-1 visa residency requirements, mistakes can lead to travel bans or rejected applications.

We built GlobalVisaMath to provide clear, high-fidelity compliance calculations without the need for logins, fees, or data harvesting. All processing is kept 100% private, running locally inside your browser.

Trusted & Transparent Formulas

Our algorithms follow public legal guidelines and governmental instructions strictly. Below are the official sources for our models:

  • Schengen 90/180 Rules: Follows the European Parliament's Regulation (EU) 2016/399 (Schengen Borders Code) governing short-stay rolling limits.
  • Canadian CRS score: Follows the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) Ministerial Instructions for Comprehensive Ranking System criteria.
  • Green Card renewal: Built around the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) Form I-90 physical filing constraints and checklist requirements.
  • J-1 Visa restrictions: Evaluated using the United States Department of State (USDOS) Bureau of Educational and Cultural Affairs directives on active DS-2019 validation and J1 exchange program rules.

Privacy-First Architecture

We do not collect, store, or share your travel dates, passport profiles, or personal data. The site runs entirely in static HTML/JS. Because your inputs never leave your computer, your travel history and personal information remain completely confidential.
